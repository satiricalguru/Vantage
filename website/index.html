Why Vantage

  1. 01

    Every display, its own world

    Assign a different wallpaper to each monitor. Hot-plug a screen and Vantage picks up right where you left off.

  2. 02

    4K that sips power

    Range-request streaming straight from disk and automatic battery saver keep fans quiet and frames smooth.

  3. 03

    Bring your own

    Drag in MP4, MOV, WebM, PNG, JPG or WebP — or drop files in a watched folder and they appear instantly.

  4. 04

    Generative canvases

    Procedural, cursor-reactive scenes rendered live on your GPU. Nothing to download, nothing to repeat.

  5. 05

    Lives in your menu bar

    Pause, swap or tune playback from the tray. Hide the Dock icon and forget it is running.
